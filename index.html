Arithmetic Sequence Adventure

Journey Through Santa Fe, Romblon

Explore local landmarks, solve sequence puzzles, and earn badges across 5 exciting destinations!

THE MISSION

Welcome, Explorer!

You are a young explorer traveling across the beautiful municipality of Santa Fe, Romblon.

Every landmark contains a mathematical mystery governed by arithmetic sequences. Solve each challenge to continue your journey. Be careful—you only have 3 lives to complete all 5 locations!

OBJECTIVES

Learning Targets

At the end of this adventure, you should be able to:

  • Identify arithmetic sequences.
  • Determine the common difference ($d$).
  • Find missing terms in sequences.
  • Apply arithmetic sequences to real-life situations in Santa Fe.

📖 How to Play

1. Observe the numbers in each sequence carefully.

2. Calculate the common difference ($d$) by finding the change between consecutive numbers.

3. Enter the correct missing term to complete the sequence and move to the next landmark.

4. Use hints if you get stuck, but watch your lives!
